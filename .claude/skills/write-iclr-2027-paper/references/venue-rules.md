# ICLR 2027 Venue Rules

Use this reference for submission-readiness and compliance work. It records official guidance checked on 2026-09-02. Re-open the official pages before making time-sensitive claims or certifying a submission.

## Hard submission constraints

- ICLR 2027 is double blind. The main paper and supplementary material must not reveal author identity. Related author papers on arXiv may be cited, but self-citations must use third-person wording.
- Use the official ICLR 2027 LaTeX style files. Do not alter style files, margins, font sizes, or other layout parameters to evade the page limit.
- The initial submission allows at most 9 pages of main text. The discussion/rebuttal revision and camera-ready allow 10 pages. References are excluded. Appendices may be unlimited and may follow the bibliography, but reviewers are not required to read them.
- A genuine abstract is due 2026-09-18 at 23:59 Anywhere on Earth; the full paper is due 2026-09-25 at 23:59 Anywhere on Earth. No authors may be added after the abstract deadline. Revalidate dates before acting.
- The dual-submission, author-quota, reciprocal-reviewing, OpenReview-profile, sanctions, and withdrawal rules can cause desk rejection. Check the current author guide rather than summarizing them from memory.

## Required and recommended statements

- **AI use statement: required.** It is outside the page limit and must also be reflected in the submission form. The disclosure must truthfully cover how generative AI was used. Authors remain responsible for all content and must verify AI-assisted work.
- The official AI policy requires disclosure for substantive research tasks such as synthetic-data generation, conceptual or theoretical development, mathematical claims or proofs, hypothesis work, methodology or experiment design, method implementation, translation, data cleaning/reformatting, qualitative analysis, and result interpretation. It recommends disclosure for writing, editing, literature work, code, figures, brainstorming, titles, structure, and related assistance. Consult the policy for the current full lists and boilerplate.
- **Ethics statement: conditional and recommended when relevant.** Put it at the end of the main text before references. It is excluded from the page limit and should be at most one page. Address concrete concerns such as human subjects or annotators, dataset release and licensing, privacy, bias/fairness, harmful uses, conflicts, legal compliance, and research integrity. All authors must read and acknowledge the Code of Ethics during submission whether or not a statement is included.
- **Reproducibility statement: strongly encouraged.** Use a paragraph at the end of the main text before references. It is excluded from the page limit. Point readers to the precise main-paper, appendix, code, data, proof, and supplementary resources that support reproduction; do not use it as a substitute for essential methodological detail.
- Anonymous code and supplementary artifacts are encouraged. Any external repository or demonstration used during review must preserve anonymity.

## Compliance audit

Before calling a draft submission-ready, verify:

1. The compiled PDF uses the official template and stays within the applicable main-text limit.
2. Authors, affiliations, acknowledgments, repositories, metadata, PDF properties, file names, supplementary material, and self-references do not reveal identity.
3. All material needed to assess the central claims is in the main paper; appendix dependencies are signposted and nonessential to the first-pass decision.
4. The AI use statement is complete and consistent with the actual workflow and submission form.
5. Ethics risks are assessed and documented where appropriate.
6. The reproducibility statement points to concrete resources and the methods report what is needed to verify results.
7. Citations are complete, third-person where required, and compatible with the dual-submission policy.
8. Author profiles, quotas, reciprocal-reviewing obligations, author list, abstract, and deadlines have been checked by the authors against the current official guide.
