# Improve ICLR Related Work

Related work establishes a credible novelty boundary by connecting the problem and gap to how the contribution differs from the closest alternatives, the evidence for that distinction, its scope, and its significance. Preserve supported scientific meaning, citations, notation, and local voice; use `write-iclr-2027-paper` for whole-paper consistency, anonymity guidance, or current venue requirements.

## Input check

Determine whether the request is diagnosis, restructuring or revision, or drafting. Obtain the claimed novelty boundary, supplied sources and comparison evidence, and the paper’s method and scope; identify missing author-supplied context.

## Acceptance-impact audit

1. Organize work around distinctions needed to establish novelty, not chronology or disconnected citation clusters.
2. Compare the closest alternatives by research question, assumptions, method, evidence, and scope.
3. Use precise, respectful limitations; do not create straw-man summaries or call work “different” without a supported distinction.
4. Prefer primary sources and verify bibliographic claims against actual source material. Never invent, complete, or recommend citations from memory; label unverified claims.
5. Preserve anonymous third-person self-citation conventions, routing current anonymity rules to `write-iclr-2027-paper`. Keep prerequisites in background and new approach details in method.

## Revise

Restructure only as needed to make closest-work comparisons fair and assessable. State distinctions only where the manuscript and sources support them; separate substantive revisions from citation or evidence gaps requiring author action.

## Output

Return `Revised section` only when requested, followed by `Material changes`, `Evidence gaps`, and `Reviewer risks`.
