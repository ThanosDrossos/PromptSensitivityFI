# Improve ICLR Introduction

The introduction establishes the acceptance case by moving from the problem and literature-supported gap to verifiable contributions, their evidence and scope, and significance for learning-representations readers. Preserve supported meaning, notation, citations, and local voice; use `write-iclr-2027-paper` for whole-paper consistency or current venue requirements.

## Input check

Determine whether the request is diagnosis, revision, or drafting. Obtain the research question, closest-work evidence, claimed contributions, downstream support, and intended readership; identify any missing author-supplied context.

## Acceptance-impact audit

1. Move quickly from context to the exact problem and a literature-supported unsolved gap.
2. State each contribution as a verifiable outcome, not an activity such as “we investigate” or “we conduct experiments.” Pair every headline contribution with later evidence and the scope in which it holds.
3. Make significance to learning-representations readers explicit without hype; distinguish it from unsupported claims about broad deployment contexts.
4. Mark an unsupported novelty boundary as an evidence or citation gap, not a prose problem.
5. Identify tutorial prerequisite material that belongs in background or preliminaries.

## Revise

Reorder or rewrite only enough to make the problem–gap–contribution–evidence chain clear. Preserve claims the paper can support, preview the relevant evidence, and separate substantive revisions from evidence gaps requiring author action; never manufacture citations, novelty, or results.

## Output

Return `Revised section` only when requested, followed by `Material changes`, `Evidence gaps`, and `Reviewer risks`.
