# Improve ICLR Reproducibility and Ethics

Reproducibility and responsibility support the evidence-to-scope-to-significance links in the problem–gap–contribution–evidence–scope–significance chain: readers must be able to locate evidence and assess material risks. Ethics and broader-impact statements are conditional on actual risks. Preserve supplied scientific meaning, notation, citations, and local voice; use `write-iclr-2027-paper` for current statement placement, page treatment, AI-use policy, whole-paper consistency, or venue requirements.

## Input check

Identify diagnosis, revision, or drafting. Obtain verified artifact locations and access constraints, essential method detail, data provenance and licenses, applicable risk assessment and mitigations, release decisions, and actual AI-use workflow. Name missing facts rather than creating factual boilerplate.

## Acceptance-impact audit

1. Map reproducibility claims to actual main-paper, appendix, code, data, proof, configuration, and artifact locations; ensure they supplement rather than replace essential method detail.
2. Do not infer that code, data, models, checkpoints, or configurations will be released, or that consent, license approval, or a mitigation exists.
3. Where applicable, assess stakeholders; data provenance/license; privacy; bias/fairness; misuse; safety; environmental burden; human subjects or annotators; and release trade-offs.
4. For every material risk, report pathway, likelihood and severity where supportable, mitigation actually taken, residual risk, and the author decision still needed.
5. Make AI-use disclosure reflect the author-supplied workflow only; flag absent history instead of asserting tool use or compliance.

## Revise

Replace generic promises and blanket “no concerns” claims with verified locations, constraints, and bounded factual statements. Separate supported revisions from prose-incurable gaps requiring author evidence or judgment. Do not invent artifacts, permissions, releases, risk levels, mitigations, or disclosure history.

## Output

Return `Revised section` only when requested, followed by `Material changes`, `Evidence gaps`, and `Reviewer risks`.
