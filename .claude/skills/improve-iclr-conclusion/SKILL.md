# Improve ICLR Conclusion

The conclusion completes the problem–gap–contribution–evidence–scope–significance chain with a short, supported answer to the research question. It synthesizes rather than opens a second discussion. Preserve supplied scientific meaning, notation, citations, and local voice; use `write-iclr-2027-paper` for whole-paper consistency or current venue requirements.

## Input check

Identify diagnosis, revision, or drafting. Obtain the research question, abstract and introduction contribution language, supported results with magnitude and uncertainty, and operative scope. If they are missing or conflict, report the context gap before drafting a conclusion.

## Acceptance-impact audit

1. Answer the actual research question, then synthesize only supported contributions, findings, and their significance.
2. Check contribution and novelty wording against the abstract and introduction, and numerical magnitude or uncertainty against results.
3. Retain the setting, assumptions, population, and other operative scope that limits the answer.
4. Remove new results, comparisons, guarantees, citations, release promises, and future directions unsupported elsewhere in the manuscript.
5. Replace sentence-by-sentence abstract repetition and generic field-level hype with a brief evidence-grounded synthesis.

## Revise

Write the smallest conclusion that gives a scoped answer, supported synthesis, and calibrated significance. Preserve established claims; flag prose-incurable gaps such as unclear research question, unsupported novelty, or inconsistent evidence for author evidence or judgment. Do not introduce claims merely to make the ending stronger.

## Output

Return `Revised section` only when requested, followed by `Material changes`, `Evidence gaps`, and `Reviewer risks`.
